# AC Service Call Troubleshooter

A simple decision tree for your HVAC company to handle AC service calls over the phone and reduce unnecessary truck rolls.

## How to Use This Guide
When a customer calls:
1. Follow the steps in order.
2. Ask the customer to do the easy checks.
3. Decide: Fix over the phone OR Send a tech.

---

## 1. Start Every Call With These Questions
- Exact address and phone number?
- When did the problem start? (Today, yesterday, gradual, sudden?)
- Main issue? (Choose one)
  - No power / unit not turning on
  - Running but not cooling (warm air)
  - Frozen / ice on pipes
  - Weak or no airflow
  - Strange noises, smells, or water leaking
  - Other

---

## 2. No Power / Unit Not Turning On
- Thermostat: New batteries? Set to **Cool** and fan **Auto**. Lower temp 5°F.
- Check breakers (indoor + outdoor disconnect switch). Reset once if tripped.
- Replace air filter if dirty.
- **Still dead?** → Schedule tech.

---

## 3. Running But Not Cooling
- Replace air filter (most common fix).
- Clear grass, leaves, debris around outdoor unit (keep 2 ft clear).
- Check for ice on the big copper pipe near the indoor unit.
- **If ice is present** → Go to Frozen section.
- **No improvement after 1 hour** → Schedule tech.

---

## 4. Frozen Unit / Ice on Coils or Pipes
**Customer instructions:**
1. Turn thermostat **OFF**.
2. Set fan to **ON** (this helps thaw).
3. Replace air filter.
4. Do **NOT** chip at the ice.
5. Wait 2–6 hours until fully thawed.
6. Turn back to Cool and test.

- If it freezes again or doesn’t cool → **Schedule tech**.

---

## 5. Weak or No Airflow
- Replace air filter.
- Make sure all room vents are open.
- Listen at furnace/air handler — is the indoor fan running?
- **Fan not running or very weak** → Schedule tech.

---

## 6. Strange Noises, Smells, or Leaks
- Burning smell, grinding noise, or water leaking inside → **Turn system OFF and schedule tech immediately**.

---

## Quick Decision Summary
**Handle over the phone (no tech needed):**
- Dead thermostat batteries
- Tripped breaker
- Dirty air filter
- Blocked outdoor unit
- Frozen coil (after thawing instructions)

**Send a tech:**
- Still not working after above steps
- Ice keeps coming back
- No power after resets
- Noises, burning smell, water leaks
- Customer is uncomfortable doing checks

---

## Tips for Your Team
- Ask customer to text you a photo of the filter and outdoor unit.
- Offer to text them the steps.
- Always remind: “Never open the unit or touch electrical parts.”
- Track how many calls you solve without rolling a truck.
